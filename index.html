What is opportunity cost?

Opportunity cost is the value of what you give up when you choose to wait or do nothing. Every delayed decision has a hidden financial cost, even if no money leaves your bank account.

How this opportunity cost calculator works

This calculator estimates how much income or revenue you lose over time by delaying action. Simply enter your monthly income, choose how long you delay, and instantly see the financial impact.

Why waiting is expensive

Waiting feels safe, but it compounds losses quietly. Whether it's delaying a career move, a business decision, or an investment, time is the most expensive resource you can waste.
